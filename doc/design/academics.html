The KIMS School Advantage

Strong Foundations from Nursery to Matric Board Honors

At KIMS School, we believe that academic excellence in Matriculation begins in the early nursery and primary years. Our child-centered curriculum combines modern educational technology with time-honored discipline and character values.

Hands-on Science & Computer Labs

Equipped with physics, chemistry, and biology experimental apparatus plus a modern IT computing lab.

Rigorous BISE Kohat Board Test Series

Chapter-wise mock exams, model papers, and handwriting/presentation drills ensuring top board ranks.

Nazra Quran, Tajweed & Tarbiyah

Integrated moral ethics, daily Duas, Quranic recitation, and character grooming from playgroup onwards.

Modern Pedagogical Method

"Every Child Is Nurtured to Lead"

Small teacher-to-student ratios, individualized attention, and active parental communication.

Simple Process

How to Enroll Your Child at KIMS

A straightforward 4-step admission journey from inquiry to first day of classes.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the fast online application form or visit our admission office on Dhoda Road, Kohat.

2

Assessment / Interview

Informal readiness assessment for Nursery/Prep, or basic placement test for Grades 1-9.

3

Document Verification

Submit candidate's B-Form, parent's CNIC, previous school leaving certificate/DMC, and photos.

4

Fee & Welcome Kit

Receive student ID card, uniform handbook, booklist, and orientation schedule.
