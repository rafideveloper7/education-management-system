How to Reach KIMS School

Conveniently situated on Dhoda Road, Dheri Banda, Kohat with broad road access and ample parking.

KIMS School Campus Dhoda Road, Dheri Banda, Kohat

From Kohat City Center

Take Bannu Road toward Dhoda Road junction; KIMS campus is situated just 7 minutes drive from the main bypass chowk.

From KDA Kohat

Direct transit via Rawalpindi Road connecting Dhoda bypass. School van pick-and-drop serves all sectors of KDA.

Visitor Parking

Dedicated secure parking space available inside the school gates for parents during admission and PTM hours.

Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions most commonly asked by prospective parents.

KIMS provides comprehensive schooling from Early Childhood Education (Playgroup, Nursery, Prep) up to 10th Class (Matriculation - SSC) with Science streams in both Biology and Computer Science tracks. We do not have 11th and 12th college classes.
Students scoring 85%+ in their previous school examinations qualify for up to 75% fee waivers, and 90%+ scorers receive 100% free tuition. In addition, sibling concessions (20% off) and orphan/need-based discounts are available upon submitting relevant documents. You can check our interactive calculator on the Admissions Page.
Yes, KIMS operates school vans covering Kohat City, KDA Phase 1 & 2, Bannu Road, Rawalpindi Road, and nearby rural union councils with verified, experienced drivers and staff escorts.
For Early Childhood (Playgroup, Nursery, Prep), admission is granted through an informal, friendly interaction with the child and parents. No formal written test is required. Simply submit the online admission form or visit our campus desk with the child's NADRA B-Form.
