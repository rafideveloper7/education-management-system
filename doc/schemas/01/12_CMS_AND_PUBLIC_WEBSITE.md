## 💡 Summary: Why this design makes decision-making easy

1. **Zero Developer Costs for Content**: Changing the phone number, adding a new photo album, or updating fee FAQs requires zero programming.
2. **Instant Rebranding**: If the school decides to change its logo or color palette, the entire website updates instantly from the Admin Panel.
3. **Structured Inquiries**: Messages from the public "Contact Us" form go directly into the Admin dashboard for easy reply tracking.
