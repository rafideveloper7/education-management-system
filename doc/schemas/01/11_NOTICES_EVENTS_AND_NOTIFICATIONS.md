## 📢 2. School Notices (`notices`)

Official circulars and announcements published by the administration.

### Real-World Notice Example:
```
Title:            Winter Uniform Transition Notice
Target Audience:  All Students & Parents
Publish Date:     15 October 2026
Is Pinned to Top: YES
Message:          "Starting Monday 1st November, all students are required to wear the official navy blue winter sweater."
Attached Files:   Uniform_Guidelines_2026.pdf
```

## 💡 Summary: Why this design makes decision-making easy

1. **No Information Overload**: Parents only see notices relevant to their children's grade, rather than getting spammed by circulars for other classes.
2. **Pinned Notices**: Important emergencies or fee deadlines can be pinned to stay at the very top of everyone's dashboard.
3. **Multi-Channel Alerts**: Crucial alerts (like sudden weather holidays) are sent via SMS, Email, and mobile app notifications simultaneously.
