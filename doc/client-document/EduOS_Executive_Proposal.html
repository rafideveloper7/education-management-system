Pakistani Schools & Colleges Ke Liye Mukammal Digital System

EduOS Ke 5 Asal Modules (Aasan Roman Urdu Mein)

Baghair kisi mushkil English ke, seedha aur aasan lafzon mein samjhein ke har user ke liye system mein kya sahulat mojood hai.

1. Admin (Principal) 2. Teacher (Ustaad) 3. Student (Talib-e-Ilm) 4. Parent (Walidain) 5. School Website

Ahem Rule: Har bande ka apna alag panel hai. Teacher ko Admin ka hisab nahi dikhta, Student ko doosre bache ke marks ya fees nahi dikhti, aur Parent sirf apne bache ka record dekh sakta hai. Pura system 100% mehfooz hai.

Module 01 • Sab Se Bara Control
Istemal Karne Wale: School Owner / Principal / Head Office

1. Admin Module (School Malik Aur Principal Ka Control Room)

Pore school ko chalane ka asal control room yahi hai. Yahan se fees ka hisab kitab, naye dakhlay (admissions), asatza ki classes, bachon ke report cards, haziri, aur school ki website bina kisi web developer ke khud control hoti hai.

1.1 Dashboard

Live School Summary (Ek Nazar Mein Sab Dekhein)

Subah aate hi Principal ko ek screen par pore school ki taaza report mil jati hai:

  • Kul kitne bache dakhil hain aur kitne teachers mojood hain.
  • Aaj kitne bache aur teachers hazir hain aur kitne ghair hazir.
  • Is maheenay kitni fees aayi aur kitni baaqi (arrears) hai.
  • Kitni chutti ki darkhwastein (leave applications) pending hain.
  • Naye dakhlay ki kitni forms review ke liye aayi hain.
Koi register kholne ki zaroorat nahi
1.2 Fees System

Fees Aur Bank Challan Ka Hisab Kitab

Paper slips aur hath se hisab karne ki ghaltiyan khatam:

  • 3-Part Bank Challan: 1 click par Bank, School aur Parent copy wala pakka challan print karein.
  • Fees Heads: Tuition fee, admission fee, exam fee, transport fee khud set karein.
  • Riyayat / Discount: Bhai/behan (sibling) discount aur yateem/merit discount ka pakka record.
  • Baqaya Jaat (Arrears): Pichli baaqi fees khud aglay maheenay ke challan mein shamil ho jati hai.
  • Defaulters List: 1 button daba kar un bachon ki list nikalein jinki fees baaqi hai aur unke parents ko SMS bhejein.
Fees mein chori aur ghalti ka 100% khatma
1.3 Student Profile

Bache Ka Mukammal Record (Ek Screen Par)

Almariyon mein purani files dhoondne ki zaroorat nahi. Sirf bache ka naam ya Roll Number likhein aur sab kuch samnay:

  • Bache ka naam, photo, class, section, walid ka naam aur phone number.
  • Pore saal ki haziri (kitne din hazir, kitne din ghair hazir).
  • Fees ka pura hisab (kitni ada ki, kitni baaqi hai, kab receipts bani).
  • Exam ke marks, grades, test result aur report cards.
  • Upload kiye gaye documents (B-Form, birth certificate waghera).
1 second mein bache ki mukammal tareekh samnay
1.4 New Admission

Naye Dakhlay (Online Admissions)

Website se aane wali dakhla forms ko seedha approve ya reject karein:

  • Walidain ghar baithe website par form bharte hain aur photo upload karte hain.
  • Principal office mein sari forms ki list aa jati hai.
  • Admission test aur interview ke marks enter karein.
  • Jaise hi "Approve" par click karein, bache ka student account khud ban jata hai, Roll Number mil jata hai aur class assign ho jati hai.
Dobara hath se data likhne ki koi zaroorat nahi
1.5 Classes & Subjects

Classes, Sections Aur Subjects Ki Setting

School ke naye saal (Session) ka pura structure tayyar karein:

  • Naya saal (jaise 2026-2027) shuru karein aur purani class se bache agli class mein promote karein.
  • Classes aur unke sections (Section A, Section B) banayein.
  • Mazameen (Subjects) dalein (Urdu, English, Math, Science waghera).
  • Har class ke liye In-charge Teacher aur Subject Teacher muqarrar karein.
Pura school rules ke mutabiq chalay ga
1.6 Exams & Results

Imtehanat Aur DMC / Report Card

Hath se result banate waqt marks ki ghaltiyan aur calculator ka dard khatam:

  • Term exams, Mid-term, aur Final exam ki date sheet banayein.
  • Roll Number slip bache ke photo ke sath print karein.
  • Teachers marks enter karte hain; system khud percentage, Grade (A+, A, B) aur class mein Position calculate karta hai.
  • School ke logo aur Principal ke sign ke sath khubsurat DMC / Report Card 1-click par print karein.
100% sahi math aur computer generated DMC
1.7 Attendance Control

Pore School Ki Haziri Ka Nigran

Principal office se hi dekh sakein ke kis class ki haziri lag gayi aur kahan der hai:

  • Dekhein kis teacher ne subah 9 baje tak apni class ki haziri nahi lagayi.
  • Jo bache bina bataye chutti par hain unki list samnay aa jati hai.
  • Medical leave ya Principal ki ijazat se chutti ko "Approved Leave" mark karein.
  • Imtehan se pehle 75% haziri ki sharait check karein.
Bunk marne wale bachon ki foran nishandehi
1.8 Darkhwastein

Darkhwastein Aur Shikayat Desk

Kaghazi darkhwastein jo mez par gum ho jati theen, ab sab online aayengi:

  • Teachers ki chutti ki darkhwastein (Leave application).
  • Walidain ki taraf se bache ki bimari ki chutti ya koi masla.
  • Bache ki fees riyayat ya certificate ki darkhwast.
  • Principal 1-click se "Approved" ya "Rejected" karein aur bache/parent ko foran notification mil jaye.
Har darkhwast ka mukammal digital record
1.9 ID Card

Student Aur Teacher ID Card Generator

Bahar printing press walo ke chakkar aur mahino ka intezar khatam:

  • Admission hote hi bache ke photo aur data se card khud tayyar ho jata hai.
  • Card par School Logo, Naam, Roll No, Class, Walid ka Phone aur QR Code mojood hota hai.
  • 1 bache ka card print karein ya puri class ke cards 1 sath PDF mein print karein.
  • Teachers aur Staff ke official cards bhi isi tarah foran print hotay hain.
Standardized aur professional cards foran tayyar
1.10 Website Control

Website Ko Khud Badalna (Baghair Developer)

Choti si notice ya chutti ka elan lagane ke liye web developer ko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi:

  • School ka naam, logo, phone number aur address Admin panel se badlein.
  • Website ke rang (Theme colors) aur banners khud tabdeel karein.
  • School ke events, functions aur annual sports day ki tasweerein gallery mein dalein.
  • Chutti ka notice ya admission ka elan 1 minute mein website par chalayein.
School website par aapka 100% zati control
1.11 Audit & Security

Chori Aur Tampering Ka Mukammal Pata

Koi bhi banda chupke se kisi bache ke marks ya fees ka hisab nahi badal sakta:

  • Agar kisi clerk ne fees mein discount diya ya kisi bache ke marks badlay, to foran record ban jata hai.
  • System mein time, tareekh, computer ka IP aur badalne wale ka naam save hota hai.
  • Pehle kya value thi aur nayi kya dali gayi, dono record mein rehti hain.
Principal ko har tabdeeli ki khabar
1.12 SMS & Notices

Pore School Ko Notice Aur SMS Bhejna

Ahem elanat foran hazaron parents aur teachers tak pohonchein:

  • Barsaat ya achanak chutti ka elan 1 click par sab parents ke mobile par.
  • Sirf kisi ek class (jaise Class 10) ke parents ko meeting ka message.
  • Fees jama karwane ka aakhri tareekh ka yaad-dihani (reminder) message.
  • Staff meeting ka notice sirf teachers ke panels par display karna.
Parcha bhej kar bacho ke hath notice dene ka dor khatam
Module 02 • Asatza Ka Panel
Istemal Karne Wale: Class Teachers & Subject Teachers

2. Teacher Module (Ustaad / Asatza Ka Classroom Panel)

Yeh panel teachers ke liye nihayat aasan banaya gaya hai taake unka waqt kaghazi kamo mein zaya na ho. Teacher sirf apni class aur apne mazmoon (subject) ko dekh sakta hai. 30 second mein haziri, homework dena, aur test ke marks charhana yahan se hota hai.

2.1 Timetable

Aaj Ka Timetable Aur Classes

Teacher apna phone ya laptop khole to samnay aaj ka din saf dikhai deta hai:

  • Kaun sa period kis class aur kis room mein parhana hai.
  • Agli class shuru hone mein kitne minute baaqi hain.
  • Aaj kaun si assignment bacho se wasool karni hai.
  • School Principal ki taraf se aane wale ahem circulars.
Har ustaad ka apna personalized timetable
2.2 Haziri

30 Second Mein Haziri (Smart Attendance)

Class mein 15 minute roll number bol kar waqt zaya karne ki zaroorat nahi:

  • Screen par sub bache pehle se "Present" hotay hain.
  • Teacher sirf un bachon ke naam par tap karta hai jo bench par nahi baithe (Absent).
  • "Submit" dabate hi ghair hazir bache ke walid ke mobile par foran SMS chala jata hai.
  • Agar bacha der se aaya ho to "Late" mark karne ka option mojood hai.
Padhayi ka waqt bachay aur haziri foran lagay
2.3 Homework

Homework Dena Aur Check Karna

Bache ab yeh bahana nahi bana sakte ke "Sir mujhe diary ka pata nahi tha":

  • Teacher class aur subject select karke homework likhta hai aur aakhri tareekh (Deadline) set karta hai.
  • Worksheet, sawaalat ki PDF ya photo sath attach kar sakta hai.
  • Walidain aur bache ke phone par homework foran show ho jata hai.
  • Bache online kaam jama karwate hain; teacher marks deta hai aur remarks likhta hai.
Diary gum hone ka jhagda hamesha ke liye khatam
2.4 Marks Entry

Imtehan Ke Numbers Enter Karna

Kaghaz ke award list banaye baghair seedha numbers computer mein dalein:

  • Teacher ko sirf uski apni class aur subject ki list dikhti hai.
  • Agar paper 100 number ka hai to ghalti se bhi 105 enter nahi ho sakta (Auto validation).
  • Aadha hisab karke "Save Draft" karein aur aglay din baaqi enter karein.
  • Mukammal hone par "Submit to Principal" karein; iske baad marks lock ho jatay hain.
Calculator ki zaroorat nahi, system khud total karega
2.5 Teacher Leave

Ustaad Ki Chutti Ki Darkhwast

Teacher ko chutti chahiye to kaghaz likhne ke bajaye mobile se apply karein:

  • Bimari (Medical) ya zaroori kaam (Casual leave) ki tareekh select karein aur waja likhein.
  • Doctor ki parchi ya attachment sath laga sakein.
  • Principal ke phone par foran notification chali jati hai aur wo approve karta hai.
  • Teacher ko pata chal jata hai ke uski kitni chuttiyan saal mein baaqi hain.
Izzat ke sath ba-zabtah digital darkhwast
2.6 Faculty Profile

Teacher Digital ID Card Aur Record

Teacher ka apna official service record aur identity card:

  • Employee ID, ohda (Designation), taaleem (Qualification) aur joining date.
  • School ka official digital card QR code ke sath mojood hota hai.
  • Teacher apna card kisi bhi waqt view, download ya print kar sakta hai.
Professional staff identity hamesha mobile mein
Module 03 • Talib-e-Ilm Ka Panel
Istemal Karne Wale: Enrolled Students (Bache)

3. Student Module (Talib-e-Ilm / Bache Ka Learning Portal)

Yeh panel bache ke liye tayyar kiya gaya hai taake wo apni parhayi, homework, exam timetable, aur result par poori tawajju de sake. Bacha kisi doosre bache ka data ya fees nahi dekh sakta, sirf apna record dekh sakta hai.

3.1 Dashboard

Bache Ka Daily Study Dashboard

Login hote hi bache ko samnay apni parhayi ka khulasa nazar aata hai:

  • Aaj kaun si class kis waqt hai aur ustaad ka naam kya hai.
  • Kaun sa homework kal jama karwana zaroori hai.
  • Pore mahinay ki haziri ka percentage (kitne din hazir raha).
  • Taaza aane wale test ke numbers aur teacher ka shabash / feedback.
Bacha parhayi mein hamesha up-to-date
3.2 Homework Desk

Homework Download Aur Jama Karwana

Bache har mazmoon ka kaam alag alag dekh aur jama karwa sakte hain:

  • English, Math, Science ka kaam alag alag tabs mein dekhein.
  • Teacher ki bheji hui notes, PDFs aur worksheets download karein.
  • Apne hal kiye hue kaam ki photo ya PDF upload karein.
  • Dekhein ke teacher ne check kar ke kitne number diye aur kya note likha.
Kaam mukammal hone ka pakka saboot
3.3 Imtehan

Date Sheet, Roll Number Aur Result

Imtehan ki tayyari aur result dekhna ab bilkul aasan hai:

  • Paper ki Date Sheet (kis din kaun sa paper hai) mobile par dekhein.
  • Imtehan hall mein le jane ke liye Roll Number Slip print karein.
  • Result aane par har subject ke marks, total percentage aur Grade dekhein.
  • Official Report Card / DMC download karein.
Result card kabhi gum nahi hoga
3.4 Haziri Chart

Bache Ka Haziri Record

Bacha khud dekh sakta hai ke wo kitne din school aaya hai:

  • Calendar par sabz rang (Hazir) aur surkh rang (Ghair Hazir) saaf nazar aata hai.
  • Agar haziri 75% se kam hone lage to bache ko warning message dikhta hai.
  • Bacha record mein koi tabdeeli nahi kar sakta (Read-only security).
Imtehan mein baithne ki sharait par nazar
3.5 Darkhwast

Bache Ki Darkhwast Aur Shikayat

Bacha school administration ya class teacher ko ba-zabtah darkhwast bhej sakta hai:

  • Chutti ki darkhwast (Leave application).
  • Character certificate ya bonafide certificate mangne ki request.
  • Agar school mein koi bacha tang kar raha ho to private complaint darj karwana.
  • Principal ka jawab aur faisla panel par check karna.
Bache ki baat seedha management tak pohonchti hai
3.6 Digital Card

Bache Ka Digital Student Card

Student ID card hamesha bache ke mobile par mojood rehta hai:

  • School gate par dikhane ke liye photo wala digital identity card.
  • Library se kitabein lene ke liye bar code aur QR code.
  • Card gum ho jane par kisi bhi waqt naya print nikalne ki sahulat.
Physical card gum hone par bhi entry aasan
Module 04 • Walidain Ka Portal
Istemal Karne Wale: Parents & Guardians (Maa Baap)

4. Parent Module (Walidain Ka Nigran Panel)

Walidain ke itminan aur sukoon ke liye sab se behtareen feature. Agar kisi walid ke 2 ya 3 bache mukhtalif classes mein parhte hain, to unko alag alag passwords ki zaroorat nahi. Ek hi login se sab bachon ka hisab, fees ka challan, aur haziri ka notification milta hai.

4.1 Sibling Switcher

Tamam Bachon Ka Ek Hi Login (1-Tap Switch)

Walidain ko har bache ke liye alag alag account kholne ki takleef nahi:

  • Ek walid ke agar 3 bache hain (jaise Ali Class 9 mein, Sara Class 5 mein, Ahmed Prep mein).
  • Screen ke upar sirf bache ke naam par tap karein aur foran uska record samnay aa jata hai.
  • Teeno bachon ki fees, haziri aur marks ek hi jagah control hote hain.
Walidain ki sab se bari pareshani hal
4.2 Ghair Haziri Alert

Foran Ghair Haziri Ka SMS / Alert

Bacha school se bunk maray ya raste mein ruk jaye to walid ko foran khabar:

  • Subah haziri lagte hi agar bacha ghair hazir ho to foran SMS chala jata hai.
  • Walidain ko mahinon baad pata chalne ke bajaye usi waqt 30 minute mein pata chal jata hai.
  • Bache ki safety mehfooz rehti hai aur laparwahi khatam hoti hai.
Bache ki hifazat aur mukammal itminan
4.3 Bank Challan

Fees Ka Challan Download Karna

Fees ki kaghazi slip gum ho jaye to school ke chakkar lagane ki zaroorat nahi:

  • Monthly bank challan (3-part) ghar baithe 1 click par download aur print karein.
  • Dekhein ke tuition fee kitni hai, baqaya kitna hai aur aakhri tareekh kya hai.
  • Pichlay tamam maheeno ki ada ki hui fees ki receipts ka record mehfooz rehta hai.
  • Bhai/behan ka mila hua discount challan par saaf show hota hai.
Pura hisab saf aur transparent
4.4 DMC Card

Bache Ka Result Aur Report Card

Parents meeting ka intezar kiye baghair bache ki karkardagi par nazar:

  • Rozana homework check ho raha hai ya nahi, aur ustaad ne kya remarks diye.
  • Monthly test aur term exams ke marks aur position foran mobile par dekhein.
  • Official DMC / Report Card PDF mein download karein.
  • Pata chal jata hai bacha kis mazmoon mein kamzor hai taake waqt par tawajju di jaye.
Saal ke aakhir mein achanak fail hone ka jhatka nahi
4.5 Walid Ki Darkhwast

Bache Ki Chutti Aur Shikayat Bhejna

Teacher ko WhatsApp par raat 11 baje message karne ke bajaye ba-zabtah tareeqah:

  • Agar bacha bimar ho to walid application likh kar doctor ki parchi upload karta hai.
  • Class teacher ya Principal se meeting ki request bhej sakta hai.
  • School bus (Transport) ya kisi ustaad ke rawaiye ki shikayat darj karwana.
  • School ki taraf se aane wala official jawab ticket par nazar aata hai.
Ba-waqar aur saboot ke sath communication
4.6 School Circulars

School Circulars Aur Chuttiyon Ki List

Bache ke bag mein parcha phat jane ya gum hone ka masla khatam:

  • Mausam ki kharabi ya hakoomati chutti ka official circular foran samnay.
  • Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM) ki tareekh aur waqt.
  • Annual sports day, vacation dates aur school timings ka schedule.
Har official khabar seedha walid ke phone par
Module 05 • Aam Awam Ke Liye
Istemal Karne Wale: Naye Parents, Visitors Aur Candidates

5. Public Module (School Ki Website, Online Dakhla, Results Aur Awami Desk)

Yeh school ka digital chehra hai jo pore internet par aam awam ko dikhta hai. Iske liye kisi login ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Naye walidain school ki sahuliyat dekhte hain, online dakhla form bharte hain, admission status check karte hain, class aur roll number daal kar bache ka result dekhte hain, aur awami darkhwastein ya shikayat darj karwate hain.

5.1 Khubsurat Website

School Ka Shandar Digital Showcase

School ki shandar profile jo naye walidain ko mutasir kare:

  • Homepage: School ka banner, Principal ka paigham aur ahem karkardagi.
  • About School: Tareekh, mission aur board affiliation ki tafseelat.
  • Courses & Classes: Playgroup se Matric / FSc tak ki information.
  • Sahuliyat (Facilities): Science labs, computer lab, library aur ground ki tasweerein.
Mobile aur computer dono par zabardast display
5.2 Online Dakhla

Ghar Baithe Online Dakhla Form

Dakhlay ke dino mein school office mein lambi qataron ka khatma:

  • Naye parents website par admission policy aur fees schedule check karte hain.
  • Online form bharte hain: Bache ka naam, walid ka phone, pichla school.
  • Bache ka photo aur B-Form mobile se hi upload karte hain.
  • Form submit hote hi bache ko instant Tracking Token Number mil jata hai.
Seedha Admin Panel ke admission pipeline mein sync
5.3 Dakhla Check

Online Admissions Check (Dakhla Status)

Walidain ghar baithe apne bache ke dakhlay ki mukammal report check karein:

  • Sirf Tracking Token, B-Form number ya Mobile number daalein.
  • Status dekhein: "Form Check Ho Rahi Hai", "Test Ki Tareekh Tay Hai", ya "Dakhla Manzoor".
  • Admission test aur interview ki date sheet aur entry slip download karein.
  • Dakhla pass hone par pehli fees ka computerized Bank Challan print karein.
Bar bar school phone karne ya chakkar lagane ki zaroorat nahi
5.4 Public Results

All Public Results (Roll No & Class Search)

Board ki tarah school ki website par aam awam aur har bache ke liye online result portal:

  • Baghair Login Ke: Koi bhi bacha, walid ya rishtedar result dekh sakta hai.
  • Aasan Form: Exam Session select karein (jaise Annual 2026), Class select karein (jaise Class 9 ya 10), aur Roll Number daalein.
  • Mukammal DMC Card: Screen par bache ka naam, walid ka naam, har subject ke marks, total marks, percentage, Grade (A+, A, B) aur class position samnay aa jati hai.
  • Print / Download: School ke official stamp aur logo ke sath Result Card 1-click par print karein.
BISE Board jaisa shandar computerized result system
5.5 Awami Desk

Awami Darkhwastein Aur Shikayat Desk

Aam awam, mohallay ke log aur naye walidain bina kisi login ke darkhwast ya shikayat bhej sakte hain:

  • Category chunein: Dakhla Inquiry, Fees Masla, School Van / Transport Shikayat, ya Aam Sujhav (Suggestion).
  • Apna naam, mobile number aur tafseel likh kar submit karein.
  • System foran ek Complaint Tracking ID generate karta hai.
  • Tracking ID daal kar kisi bhi waqt check karein ke Principal office se kya jawab aaya hai.
Awami masail ka foran digital hal aur saboot
5.6 Awami Notices

Awami Khabrein, Functions Aur Gallery

School ke naye waqiyat aur shandar kamiyabiyan public ko dikhana:

  • Board position lene wale honhar bachon ki tasweerein aur mubarakbad.
  • Sports day, debate competition aur meena bazaar ki tasweerein.
  • Admission open ka banner aur aakhri tareekh ka elan.
  • School prospectus PDF mein download karne ki sahulat.
School ki achi reputation aur marketing
5.7 Rabta Desk

School Ka Pata Aur Rabta Form

School se asani se rabta karne ki tamam sahuliyat:

  • Google Map par school ki exact location aur rasta.
  • Admin office, accounts office aur admission branch ke PTCL / WhatsApp numbers.
  • Website par sawal pochne ka direct message form.
  • Naye parents school visit ka waqt (Appointment) book kar sakein.
Koi inquiry ya rabta miss nahi hota
5.8 Login Gateway

Sab Ka Ek Hi Login Darwaza (Smart Routing)

Website ke kone mein sirf ek "Portal Login" ka button hota hai:

• Principal login karega → Admin Panel khulay ga
• Teacher login karega → Teacher Panel khulay ga
• Bacha login karega → Student Panel khulay ga
• Walid login karega → Parent Panel khulay ga
System khud pehchan kar sahi panel par le jata hai
Mukhtasir Khulasa
