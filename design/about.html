Principal's Address

"We do not merely teach science; we inspire our youth to lead through knowledge."

At Kohat Institute of Modern Sciences (KIMS), our journey began with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical innovation in southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Over two decades, we have evolved from a visionary institution into a recognized center of academic excellence.

Our students consistently secure top positions in BISE Kohat board examinations, gaining admissions to top medical universities (such as KMC, AMC) and premier engineering colleges (UET, NUST, FAST). But more than test scores, we take pride in fostering critical thinking, integrity, and ethical leadership in every scholar who passes through our doors.

Foundation & Pillars

Vision, Mission & Values

Guiding principles that define our curriculum, student development, and community impact.

Our Vision

To be acknowledged as the standard of educational excellence in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, producing inquisitive thinkers, ethical scholars, and innovative scientists equipped to make meaningful global contributions.

Our Mission

To deliver rigorous, conceptually rooted secondary and higher-secondary education through advanced scientific facilities, supportive faculty, disciplined learning environments, and character-building opportunities.

Scientific Curiosity

Cultivating an investigative mindset through hands-on laboratory experiments and analytical thinking.

Moral Integrity

Emphasizing honesty, ethical conduct, and respect as core foundations of every graduate's character.

Academic Rigor

Maintaining continuous evaluation, board-aligned preparation, and demanding academic benchmarks.

Student Mentorship

Individualized student guidance ensuring emotional well-being, career clarity, and college prep.

Our Milestones

Two Decades of Progress

Key turning points in KIMS' evolution into Kohat's premier science school.

2004

Founding of KIMS

Established on Dhoda Road in Kohat with a foundational mission to provide quality science education to students across Kohat division.

2010

BISE Kohat Top Positions

Achieved our first top 3 position holders in BISE Kohat board examinations, setting a standard that has continued unbroken each year.

2016

Expanded Science & IT Wing

Constructed state-of-the-art Physics, Chemistry, and Biology practical laboratories, alongside our first specialized Computer Science & Coding lab.

2024

Modern Digital Campus & Record Scores

Surpassed 10,000 alumni, rolled out student digital portal access, and celebrated an unprecedented 98.4% board top score in BISE Kohat Matriculation examinations.
